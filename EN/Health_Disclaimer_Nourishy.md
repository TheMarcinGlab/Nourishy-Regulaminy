## 1. No Medical Device Status and No Medical Service

* **1.1. No medical services:** The Nourishy Application **is not a medical device** within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745 of the European Parliament and of the Council (MDR) and does not provide medical, physician, therapeutic advice or individual clinical dietetics diagnoses.
* **1.2. Informational character:** All information, recipe suggestions, meal plans and nutritional value calculations (including macronutrients and calorie content) contained in the Application are solely educational, recreational and supportive of a healthy lifestyle.
* **1.3. No doctor–patient relationship:** Use of the Application does not create a patient–doctor or patient–dietitian relationship between the User and Marcin Głąb.

## 2. Role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Risk of Errors

* **2.1. Limitation of the AI role:** Artificial intelligence (AI) modules used in the Application serve solely for image analysis (photos of meals, food products and receipts). Artificial intelligence does not replace human judgement.
* **2.2. AI errors and hallucinations:** Due to the probabilistic nature of AI vision models, the Application may incorrectly recognise a dish, inaccurately estimate portion weight or wrongly identify nutritional values and ingredients in a photo.
* **2.3. Duty to verify labels:** The User has an absolute obligation to verify on each occasion the composition, nutritional values and allergens against the official label placed on the physical product packaging before consumption.

## 4. Health Contraindications and Need for Consultation

* **4.1. Higher-risk groups:** Persons suffering from chronic diseases (including diabetes, coeliac disease, insulin resistance, kidney or heart disease), pregnant and breastfeeding women, persons with eating disorders (e.g. anorexia, bulimia) and persons taking regular medication have an absolute obligation to consult every dietary change with their treating physician or a clinical dietitian.
* **4.2. Emergencies:** In the event of a sudden deterioration of health, occurrence of allergy symptoms or any medical doubts, the User should immediately stop using the suggested diet and contact a physician or call the emergency number **112**.
