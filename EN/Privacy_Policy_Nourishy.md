# 🛡️ Nourishy Mobile Application Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 21, 2026

---

## 1. General provisions and Data Controller

* **1.1.** This Privacy Policy sets out the principles, purposes, legal bases, and retention periods for the processing of personal data of users of the Nourishy mobile application (application identifier: `com.nourishy.app`), hereinafter referred to as the **"Application"**.
* **1.2.** The controller of users' personal data is a natural person conducting business activity and making the Application available: **Marcin Głąb**, hereinafter referred to as the **"Controller"**.
* **1.3.** For all matters relating to the processing of personal data, privacy protection, and exercise of rights under the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), the user may contact the Controller via the dedicated and sole official email address:
  📧 **[support@nourishy.eu](mailto:support@nourishy.eu)**
* **1.4.** The Controller declares that personal data of users is processed in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR), the Polish Personal Data Protection Act, and the Act on the Provision of Electronic Services.
* **1.5. Age restriction:** The Application is intended exclusively for adults who have reached the age of **18**. The Controller does not knowingly collect, process, or store personal data, including health data, of individuals under 18 years of age. If the Controller becomes aware that an account has been registered by a minor, such account along with all associated data will be immediately and permanently deleted from production systems in real time.

## 📊 2. Categories and scope of personal data collected

To enable use of all advanced features of the Application, the Controller collects and processes the following categories of personal data:

* **2.1. Registration and authentication data:** email address, user identifier generated by the Auth0 system.
* **2.2. Profile data and health information (Special category data – Art. 9 GDPR):** weight, height, age, gender, level of physical activity, taste preferences, selected world cuisines, nutritional goals (e.g., weight reduction, muscle building), and primarily **food allergies, intolerances, and dietary exclusions**.
  > 🧬 **Legal note:** This data constitutes health-related data within the meaning of Art. 9(1) GDPR. It is processed solely on the basis of the user's express, separate, and informed consent collected during the onboarding process.
* **2.3. User content (photos and files):** photos of meals, dishes, and food products uploaded by the user via the device camera or gallery for analysis of their composition by artificial intelligence (AI). Uploaded photos may be used by the Controller to **improve and train AI models** used in the Application — on the basis of the license granted by the user when uploading content (details in the [Terms of Service](Terms_of_Service_Nourishy.md), § 4.2).
* **2.4. Application usage and content data:** information entered within the virtual refrigerator feature (owned products), shopping lists, history of generated meal plans, preferences rated using the "swipe" method, and macronutrient consumption statistics.
* **2.5. Technical and location data:** IP address, mobile device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), unique device identifier (IDFA/GAID), system language, unique push notification token, and geolocation (for synchronizing the user's market, currency, and country).
* **2.6. Transaction and purchase data:** Premium subscription status (e.g., trial version, monthly subscription, annual subscription), purchase date, expiration date, and transaction identifier generated by the RevenueCat system and mobile store.
  > 💳 **Important:** The Controller does not collect, process, or see payment card data or detailed banking data of the user — payments are handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play.

## ⚖️ 3. Purposes and legal bases for data processing (GDPR)

Personal data of users is processed by the Controller for the following purposes, on the basis of the indicated GDPR provisions:

* **3.1. Provision of electronic services (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):** Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract for the provision of services (acceptance of the Terms). This includes account registration, session maintenance (Auth0), operation of the virtual refrigerator, shopping cart, barcode scanner, and language and market synchronization based on geolocation.
* **3.2. Processing of health data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR):** Processing of profile data relating to health, physical parameters, allergies, and dietary goals for the purpose of generating personalized recipes and multi-day meal plans by artificial intelligence (AI) takes place **solely on the basis of the user's express, voluntary, and separate consent**, given by actively checking the appropriate checkbox during onboarding. Consent may be withdrawn at any time in profile settings. *Withdrawal of consent to process this data results in inability to provide AI-powered services, as regulated in § 5.5 of the Subscription Terms.*
* **3.3. Push and local notifications (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):** Processing of device tokens for sending reminders about meals, shopping, or system notifications (home screen widgets) on the basis of consent given by the user in the device operating system settings.
* **3.4. Legitimate interest of the Controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** Processing of technical and analytical data to ensure IT security of the Application, detect errors and failures (_crash reporting_), optimize system operation, conduct anonymous statistics, and defend against or pursue potential legal claims (in particular related to exclusion of medical liability).

## 🤝 4. Data recipients and processing entrustment (including AI modules)

To ensure proper and stable operation of the Application, the Controller uses specialized external providers. User data is entrusted on the basis of rigorous data processing agreements (DPA) or shared with the following entities:

* **4.1. Auth0 (Okta, Inc.):** Entity handling secure registration, login, authentication, and user session management.
* **4.2. Railway (Railway Corp.):** Entity providing secure cloud infrastructure and databases hosting the Application API and storing encrypted user data.
* **4.3. Google LLC (Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging):** Analytics tools, crash reporting, UX optimization, and infrastructure for sending push notifications and operating home screen widgets.
* **4.4. RevenueCat, Inc.:** Entity providing technical infrastructure for managing Premium subscriptions, receipt validation, and payment status verification with Apple App Store and Google Play.
* **4.5. Apple Inc. (App Store) & Google LLC (Google Play):** Distribution platform operators processing users' transaction and payment data to complete Premium subscription purchases.
* **4.6. External cloud-based AI model providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic):** To generate personalized recipes, analyze dish images from photos, and generate multi-day meal plans, user parameters (such as age, gender, weight, allergies, goals, and uploaded photos of dishes) are transmitted to external AI models.
  > 🤖 **AI security:** Data transmitted to external AI providers for a single query is **pseudonymized** (does not include the user's email address, first name, or last name). Photos uploaded by the user may additionally be used by the Controller (Marcin Głąb) to improve and train AI models within the Application, in accordance with the granted license. External providers process data only to the extent necessary to provide the service under processing agreements (DPA).

Entities located in the USA whose services the Controller uses (including Okta/Auth0, Google, RevenueCat, OpenAI) participate in the *EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework*, meaning the level of data protection in these enterprises has been recognized by the European Commission as adequate and secure.

## ⏱️ 6. Retention period for personal data

* **6.1.** Personal data linked to the User Account is processed for the entire period of account activity, i.e., for the duration of the contract for the provision of electronic services.
* **6.2.** If the user decides to delete their account (via the option in Profile -> Settings -> Delete account), their personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted in real time from the production database.
* **6.3.** After account deletion, the Controller reserves the right to retain only a strictly limited and anonymized scope of data (e.g., RevenueCat transaction identifier, login date) solely for defense against potential legal or tax claims, for the limitation period under the Polish Civil Code (maximum 6 years).
* **6.4.** Data processed on the basis of consent (e.g., health and allergy data, push notifications) is deleted immediately after withdrawal of that consent by the user or account deletion.

## ⚙️ 8. Account deletion procedure and subscriptions

* **8.1.** Initiating account deletion in the application results in automatic, immediate, and irreversible deletion of the email address, profile data (including allergies and health parameters), meal plan history, and photos from servers and databases linked to the Application.
* **8.2.** > ⚠️ **Key consumer information:** Deleting an account in the Application **does not automatically cancel an active Premium subscription** in Apple App Store or Google Play. For technical and legal reasons dependent on mobile store operators, the user has an absolute obligation to cancel the subscription independently in device settings before deleting the account. Otherwise, charges will continue to be levied by Apple/Google.

* **9.1.** The Controller applies advanced technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of violation of users' rights or freedoms.
* **9.2.** All communication between the mobile Application and server infrastructure (API hosted on Railway) is fully encrypted using the modern SSL/TLS protocol (HTTPS).

* **9.4.** Health-related data (allergies, weight, height) is subject to additional pseudonymization within the database, and access is limited to authorized persons bound by full confidentiality obligations.
