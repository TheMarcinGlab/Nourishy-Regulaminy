# 🛡️ Privacy Policy of the Nourishy Mobile Application

**Last updated:** 23 October 2026

---

## 1. General Provisions and Data Controller

* **1.1.** This Privacy Policy sets out the rules, purposes, legal bases and retention periods for the processing of personal data of users of the Nourishy mobile application (application identifier: `com.nourishy.app`), hereinafter referred to as the **"Application"**.
* **1.2.** The controller of personal data is a natural person who does not operate a registered business (unregistered business activity within the meaning of the Polish Entrepreneurs' Law): Marcin Głąb, hereinafter referred to as the "Controller".
* **1.3.** The Controller may be contacted by e-mail at: support@nourishy.eu
* **1.4.** The Controller declares that personal data of users is processed in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR), the Polish Personal Data Protection Act and the Act on Providing Services by Electronic Means.
* **1.5. Age restriction:** The Application is intended exclusively for adults who have reached the age of **18**. The Controller does not knowingly collect, process or store personal data, including health data, of persons under the age of 18. If the Controller becomes aware that an account has been registered by a minor, such account together with all associated data will be immediately and permanently deleted from production systems in real time.

---

## 📊 2. Categories and Scope of Personal Data Collected

In order to enable the use of all advanced features of the Application, the Controller collects and processes the following categories of personal data:

* **2.1. Registration and authentication data:** e-mail address, user identifier generated by the Auth0 system.
* **2.2. Profile data and health information (Special category data – Art. 9 GDPR):** weight, height, age, sex, physical activity level, taste preferences, selected world cuisines, nutritional goals (e.g. weight loss, muscle building) and, above all, **food allergies, intolerances and dietary exclusions**.
  > 🧬 **Legal note:** This data constitutes health data within the meaning of Art. 9(1) GDPR. It is processed solely on the basis of the user's explicit, separate and informed consent collected during the onboarding process.
* **2.3. User content:** meal photos submitted for AI analysis are processed solely in real time for the purpose of fulfilling the request. The Controller does not use users' private photos to train public AI models. Details are set out in the Nourishy Terms of Service, § 4.2.
* **2.4. Usage and in-app content data:** information entered within the virtual fridge feature (owned products), shopping lists, history of generated meal plans, preferences rated by the "swipe" method and macronutrient intake statistics.
* **2.5. Technical and location data:** IP address, mobile device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), anonymous installation identifier (IDFV), system language, unique push notification token and geolocation (for market, currency and country synchronisation).
* **2.6. Transactional and purchase data:** Premium subscription status (e.g. trial, monthly subscription, annual), purchase date, expiry date and transaction identifier generated by the RevenueCat system and the mobile store.
  > 💳 **Important:** The Controller does not collect, process or see payment card data or detailed bank account details of the user – payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

## ⚖️ 3. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Users' personal data is processed by the Controller for the following purposes, on the basis of the indicated GDPR provisions:

* **3.1. Provision of services by electronic means (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):** Processing is necessary for the performance of the service agreement (acceptance of the Terms). This includes account registration, session maintenance (Auth0), operation of the virtual fridge, shopping cart, barcode scanner and language and market synchronisation based on geolocation.
* **3.2. Processing of health data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR):** Processing of profile data relating to health, physical parameters, allergies and dietary goals is based on the User's explicit consent given during registration/onboarding. Providing this data and giving consent is voluntary, but **constitutes a necessary condition for the provision of the Application's services**. Refusal to give consent prevents account creation and use of the Application. Withdrawal of consent at any time is equivalent to the Controller's inability to continue providing services and entails the need to delete the User's Account.
* **3.3. Sending push and local notifications (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):** Processing of device tokens for the purpose of sending reminders about meals, shopping or system notifications (home screen widgets) on the basis of the user's consent given in the device's operating system settings.
* **3.4. Controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** Processing of technical and analytical data to ensure the IT security of the Application, detect errors and failures (_crash reporting_), optimise system performance, compile anonymous statistics, and defend against or pursue potential legal claims (in particular those related to the medical liability disclaimer).

## 🤝 4. Data Recipients and Entrusted Processing (Including AI Modules)

To ensure the proper and stable functioning of the Application, the Controller uses specialised external providers. User data is entrusted under rigorous data processing agreements (DPAs) or disclosed to the following entities:

* **4.1. Auth0 (Okta, Inc.):** Entity handling secure registration, login, authentication and user session management.
* **4.2. Railway (Railway Corp.):** Entity providing secure cloud infrastructure and databases on which the Application's API is hosted and encrypted user data is stored.
* **4.3. Google LLC (Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging):** Analytics tools, crash reporting, UX optimisation and infrastructure for push notifications and home screen widgets.
* **4.4. RevenueCat, Inc.:** Entity providing technical infrastructure for managing Premium subscriptions, receipt validation and payment status verification with the Apple App Store and Google Play.
* **4.5. Apple Inc. (App Store) & Google LLC (Google Play):** Operators of distribution platforms that process users' transactional and payment data in order to complete Premium subscription purchases.
* **4.6. External cloud AI model providers (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic):** For the purpose of image analysis (photos of meals, food products and receipts) submitted by the User, pseudonymised image data is transmitted to external AI model providers.
  > 🤖 **AI Security:** Data transmitted to external AI providers for photo analysis is **pseudonymised** (it does not contain the User's e-mail address, first name or surname). External providers process submitted images solely in real time for the purpose of fulfilling the request and do not use them to train their own public models.

## 🌐 5. Transfers of Data Outside the European Economic Area (EEA)

* **5.1.** Due to the use of global technology providers located in the United States (USA), users' personal data may be transferred and stored outside the EEA.
* **5.2.** The Controller ensures that data transfers take place in a fully lawful and secure manner. Entities located in the USA whose services the Controller uses (including Okta/Auth0, Google, RevenueCat, OpenAI) participate in the *EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework*, meaning that the level of data protection in those enterprises has been recognised by the European Commission as adequate and secure.
* **5.3.** Where the *Data Privacy Framework* programme does not apply, the basis for transfer is the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, supplemented by advanced technical measures (encryption of data at rest and in transit).

## ⏱️ 6. Personal Data Retention Period

* **6.1.** Personal data linked to the User's Account is processed throughout the period of account activity, i.e. for the duration of the agreement for the provision of services by electronic means.
* **6.2.** If the user decides to delete the account (via the option in Profile → Settings → Delete account), their personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted in real time from the production database.
* **6.3.** After account deletion, the Controller reserves the right to retain only a strictly limited and anonymised scope of data (e.g. RevenueCat transaction identifier, login date) solely for the purpose of defending against potential legal or tax claims, for the limitation period arising from the Polish Civil Code (maximum 6 years).
* **6.4.** Data processed on the basis of consent (e.g. health and allergy data, push notifications) is deleted immediately upon withdrawal of that consent by the user or deletion of the account.

## 🔑 7. User Rights under the GDPR

Every user of the Application is entitled to the full rights arising from the GDPR, exercised by contacting **support@nourishy.eu**:

* **7.1. Right of access to data and to receive a copy (Art. 15 GDPR).**
* **7.2. Right to rectification and correction of data (Art. 16 GDPR).**
* **7.3. Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR).**
* **7.4. Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR).**
* **7.5. Right to data portability in a structured format (Art. 20 GDPR).**
* **7.6. Right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).**
* **7.7. Right to withdraw consent to the processing of sensitive data (Art. 9) at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to its withdrawal.**
* **7.8. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.** In Poland, that authority is the **President of the Personal Data Protection Office (PUODO)**, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw.

## ⚙️ 8. Account Deletion Procedure and Subscriptions

* **8.1.** Initiating the account deletion procedure in the application results in the automatic, immediate and irreversible deletion of the e-mail address, profile data (including allergies and health parameters), meal plan history and photos from the servers and databases associated with the Application.
* **8.2.**
  > ⚠️ **Key consumer information:** Self-deletion of an account in the Application **does not automatically cancel an active Premium subscription** in the Apple App Store or Google Play. For technical and legal reasons dependent on the mobile store operators, the user has an absolute obligation to cancel the subscription independently in their device settings before deleting the account. Otherwise, charges will continue to be levied by Apple/Google. To prevent automatic renewal of the subscription on iOS devices, go to: iOS Settings → [Your Name / Apple Account] → Subscriptions → Nourishy → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

## 🔒 9. Personal Data Security

* **9.1.** The Controller applies advanced technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of infringement of users' rights or freedoms.
* **9.2.** All communication between the mobile Application and the server infrastructure (API hosted on Railway) is fully encrypted using the modern SSL/TLS protocol (HTTPS).
* **9.3.** Authentication, registration and login processes are isolated and fully secured by the dedicated Auth0 platform, which prevents unauthorised access to user accounts.
* **9.4.** Health-related data (allergies, weight, height) is subject to additional pseudonymisation within the database, and access to it is granted only to authorised persons bound by a full duty of confidentiality.
